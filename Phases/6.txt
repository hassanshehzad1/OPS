### Key Learnings Is Phase Mein
- LLM Chain node **upstream fields automatically forward nahi karta** — sirf apna `output` deta hai, isliye humein `incident_id` ko manually "Attach Incident ID to Action" node se wapis jodna pada
- Do alag data streams (severity flow + action flow) ko **Merge (Combine by Matching Fields)** se jodna — common field (`incident_id`) use karke
- Duplicate `incident_id` insert error — ye actually **Phase 4 ka duplicate prevention sahi kaam kar raha tha**, bug nahi tha

### Final Architecture (Phase 6 Addition)
