### Key Principle Jo Implement Hua
> **"AI decides what to do, but deterministic code decides whether it's safe to do automatically."**

### Final Architecture
```
Final Merge (Severity + Action data)
      ↓
Decision Engine (Code node)
      ↓
  ┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
  IMMEDIATE_ESCALATE   AUTO_EXECUTE      NEEDS_APPROVAL
  (severity=critical)  (low risk +       (everything else)
                        high confidence +
                        low impact)
      ↓
Insert into PostgreSQL (with decision + decision_reason)
```
